## Selected Achievements

- Led ML-driven demand forecasting projects in the railroad and food
  distribution industries, resulting in hundreds of millions of dollars in
  increased sales and cost savings.
- Pioneered a deep learning and NLP-based model that predicted employability
  from applicant resumes, helping a human resources company become the industry
  leader in Japan.
- Streamlined operations at an international logistics container yard by
  building a deep learning and NLP model that predicts the removal time of
  imported goods.
- Developed a solution to extract critical information from high-resolution
  images of items stacked on pallets using edge devices and cloud servers.
