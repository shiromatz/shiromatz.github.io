I was born in Yokohama, Japan. I have lived in Kyoto, Kawasaki, Setagaya, and I am currently based in Washington, DC.


## Mountains and Trails

I started hiking and mountaineering when I was 15, and I still enjoy hiking, and backpacking.

- Flew hang gliders in university, including a 30-plus-mile cross-country flight.
- Thru-hiked the John Muir Trail twice.
- Hiked from the Grand Canyon South Rim to the North Rim in one day.
- Spent a few years deeply into running and trail running.
- Finished three 100-mile trail races: Grand Raid la Reunion, Ultra Trail du Mont-Blanc, and Ultra Trail Mount Fuji.
